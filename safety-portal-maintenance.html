Quick answer

What is safety portal maintenance?

Safety portal maintenance is a managed service where ATI Business Essentials completes and maintains your contractor safety prequalification in portals such as ISNetworld®, Avetta®, Veriforce®, Highwire, ComplyWorks, and Browz. ATI submits documents correctly the first time, answers questionnaires strategically, tracks recurring reporting deadlines, and responds to non-compliance notices to protect your grade. It is billed at $100 per hour.

Stay Project-Ready in Every Contractor Portal

ATI Business Essentials simplifies your compliance process. With over 20 years of industry experience, we manage and maintain your safety prequalification across ISNetworld®, Avetta®, Veriforce®, Highwire, ComplyWorks, Browz, and client-specific portals, so you are always project-ready.

What We Take Off Your Plate

Time Savings

Constant upload revisions and portal rejections can delay projects for weeks. We make sure everything is submitted correctly the first time, eliminating back-and-forth and keeping you project-ready.

Clarity & Accuracy

Technical questionnaires are easy to misinterpret, which often triggers unnecessary compliance requirements. We complete them strategically and accurately to prevent extra work and confusion.

Ongoing Maintenance

Managing monthly, quarterly, and annual reporting alongside daily operations leads to missed deadlines and lapses. We proactively track, update, and maintain every requirement so nothing slips through the cracks.

Strong Safety Grades

Non-compliance notices and score updates get buried, and scores drop quickly. We monitor and address issues immediately to protect your grade, your reputation, and your ability to win work.

Safety Portal FAQ

Which safety prequalification portals do you manage?

ISNetworld®, Avetta®, Veriforce®, Highwire, ComplyWorks, Browz, and client-specific portals.

How much does safety portal maintenance cost?

Safety portal maintenance is billed at $100 per hour. Contact ATI for a scope estimate based on how many portals and hiring clients you have.

Can you help raise a low ISNetworld or Avetta grade?

Yes. ATI monitors non-compliance notices and score changes, fixes the underlying document or questionnaire issues, and resubmits so your grade recovers and you stay approved to bid.
