Marketing FAQ

How much does Google Ads management cost at ATI?

Paid Ads Management has a $999 setup fee plus a monthly fee based on ad spend: $450/month for spend under $2,500 (dropping to $400 after 6 months), $750/month for $2,500 to $5,000 (dropping to $600), and $1,500/month above $5,000 (dropping to $1,200). Each plan includes one meeting per month.

What is the difference between Paid Ad Management and Paid Ad Support?

Paid Ad Management is done for you: ATI builds, runs, and optimizes your campaigns. Paid Ad Support is advisory: you keep running your own ads while ATI reviews campaigns, audits tracking, recommends budget and keyword changes, and trains your team.

Which ad platforms do you work with?

Google Ads is the primary platform. Paid Ad Support also covers Microsoft Ads and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads.

What is Marketing Hub?

Marketing Hub is ATI’s marketing software. It connects your website, social media, paid ads, and Leads Plus data in one dashboard, logs every marketing change, shows its impact on leads and revenue, and uses AI to recommend where to focus. It costs $299/month plus $999 onboarding.

Are your specialists Google certified?

Yes. The team holds Google Ads Search, Google Ads Display, and Google Analytics conversion optimization certifications through Google Skillshop.
