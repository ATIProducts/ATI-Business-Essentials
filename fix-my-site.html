Website Support FAQ

How much does website maintenance cost?

Fix My Site plans are $200 per month for Maintenance, $300 per month for Security, and $400 per month for Performance. One-time repairs are quoted after a quick review.

Can you remove malware from a hacked WordPress site?

Yes. ATI removes malware, closes the security holes that let it in, and hardens the site to prevent reinfection.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. There are no long-term contracts unless you want ongoing care.

Do you only work on WordPress?

WordPress is the specialty. ATI also handles hosting and domain migrations and Google setup such as Ads, indexing, and Analytics configuration. Ask about other platforms.
