Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about ATI Business Essentials: Leads Plus, Google Ads management, Marketing Hub, safety compliance, and website support.

Leads Plus

Go to the home page

Is Leads Plus a CRM?

No. Leads Plus is a focused lead and bid tracking system. It skips the contact-management bloat of a full CRM and only tracks what service businesses need: incoming leads, calls, bid amounts, job types, and win or loss status.

How does Leads Plus improve Google Ads results?

When you mark a lead as qualified, Leads Plus sends that conversion back to Google Ads along with the dollar value of the quote. Google then optimizes toward people who look like your qualified leads instead of anyone who fills out a form, which lowers cost per qualified lead over time.

How much does Leads Plus cost?

Leads Plus with a website connection is $299 per month. Leads Plus with website and call tracking is $349 per month. Both include a one-time $699 onboarding fee, unlimited users, and up to 3 edits per month. Bundling with managed Google Ads saves over 10%.

Who is Leads Plus built for?

Contractors and service businesses that quote or bid before winning a job, including roofers, scaffolding companies, general contractors, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, excavation, landscaping, and dozens of other trades across the United States.

Do I need to change my website to use Leads Plus?

No. ATI connects your existing website forms during onboarding. If you do not want to connect your site, ATI can build a standalone landing page that feeds Leads Plus for $999 setup and $65 per month.

Leads Plus

Go to the Leads Plus page

What is the difference between Leads Plus and a CRM?

A CRM manages every contact, deal stage, email sequence, and task across a company. Leads Plus only does what bid-based service businesses need: capture leads and calls, track bid amounts and status, and tag qualified jobs. That focus makes it faster to adopt and lets it feed clean conversion data to Google Ads.

What are the Leads Plus bid statuses?

Every lead moves through four statuses: New, Bidding, Won, and Lost. You can also tag any lead as a qualified opportunity, which is the signal Leads Plus sends to Google Ads.

Can Leads Plus track phone calls?

Yes. The Leads Plus + Website + Call Connection plan ($349 per month) adds call tracking, so phone leads appear in your pipeline alongside form submissions.

How many users can access Leads Plus?

Every plan includes unlimited users. Leads Plus runs in a web browser, so there is nothing to install.

What does onboarding include?

Onboarding is a one-time $699 fee that covers connecting your website forms (and calls, if selected) plus unlimited edits and modifications discussed during onboarding. After launch, each plan includes up to 3 edits per month.

Do I have to use ATI for Google Ads to use Leads Plus?

No. Leads Plus works on its own. Bundling it with ATI’s managed Google Ads saves over 10% and gives you a team that uses your qualified-lead data to optimize campaigns.

Marketing

How much does Google Ads management cost at ATI?

Paid Ads Management has a $999 setup fee plus a monthly fee based on ad spend: $450/month for spend under $2,500 (dropping to $400 after 6 months), $750/month for $2,500 to $5,000 (dropping to $600), and $1,500/month above $5,000 (dropping to $1,200). Each plan includes one meeting per month.

What is the difference between Paid Ad Management and Paid Ad Support?

Paid Ad Management is done for you: ATI builds, runs, and optimizes your campaigns. Paid Ad Support is advisory: you keep running your own ads while ATI reviews campaigns, audits tracking, recommends budget and keyword changes, and trains your team.

Which ad platforms do you work with?

Google Ads is the primary platform. Paid Ad Support also covers Microsoft Ads and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads.

What is Marketing Hub?

Marketing Hub is ATI’s marketing software. It connects your website, social media, paid ads, and Leads Plus data in one dashboard, logs every marketing change, shows its impact on leads and revenue, and uses AI to recommend where to focus. It costs $299/month plus $999 onboarding.

Are your specialists Google certified?

Yes. The team holds Google Ads Search, Google Ads Display, and Google Analytics conversion optimization certifications through Google Skillshop.

OSHA & MSHA Companion

Go to the OSHA & MSHA Companion page

What is the difference between OSHAWise and MSHAWise?

OSHAWise covers Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) standards. MSHAWise covers Mine Safety and Health Administration (MSHA) rules for mining operations. Both include regulations, interpretations, and case law.

How much do OSHAWise and MSHAWise cost?

Each subscription is $228 when purchased through ATI Business Essentials.

Who should use OSHAWise or MSHAWise?

Safety directors, compliance managers, contractors, and mine operators who need fast, reliable answers about what a regulation requires and how it has been enforced.
