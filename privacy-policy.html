Privacy Policy

Website Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 18, 2026

Introduction

ATI Business Essentials, LLC (“Company” or “We”) values your privacy and is dedicated to safeguarding it through adherence to this policy.

This policy outlines the categories of information that may be gathered from you or that you might provide during your visits to the website www.ati-businessessentials.com (our “Website”) and explains our methods for gathering, utilizing, maintaining, protecting, and sharing that information.

This policy covers information we collect:

  • Through this Website.
  • Via email, text, and other electronic communications between you and this Website.
  • By means of mobile and desktop applications downloaded from this Website, which enable dedicated non-browser-based interaction between you and this Website.
  • During your engagement with our advertising and applications on third-party websites and services, provided these applications or advertisements contain links to this policy.

This policy does not apply to information collected by:

  • Us offline or through other channels, including any other website operated by Company or any third party; or
  • Any third party, including through any application or content (including advertising) that might link to or be accessible from or through the Website.

We encourage you to carefully review this policy to understand our policies and practices regarding your information and how we handle it. If you disagree with our policies and practices, you have the option to refrain from using our Website. By accessing or using this Website, you indicate your acceptance of this privacy policy. This policy may be updated periodically (see Changes to Our Privacy Policy). Your ongoing use of this Website following any changes indicates your acceptance of those modifications, so we recommend checking the policy regularly for updates.

Children Under the Age of 16

Our Website is not designed for users under 16 years of age. Individuals under 16 are prohibited from providing any personal information to or on the Website. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children under 16. If you are under 16, please refrain from using or providing any information on this Website or through any of its features, registering on the Website, making purchases through the Website, using any interactive or public comment features of this Website, or providing any personal details to us, including your name, address, telephone number, email address, or any screen name or user name. If we discover we have collected or received personal information from a child under 16 without verifying parental consent, we will remove that information. If you suspect we might have any information from or about a child under 16, please reach out to us at info@ati-businessessentials.com.

California residents under 16 years of age may have additional rights concerning the collection and sale of their personal information. Please refer to Your State Privacy Rights for more details.

Information We Collect About You and How We Collect It

We gather various types of information from and about users of our Website, including information:

  • That can personally identify you, such as your name, mailing address, email address, phone number, or any other identifier that enables online or offline contact (“personal information”);
  • That relates to you but doesn’t individually identify you, and/or
  • Concerning your internet connectivity, devices used to access our Website, and usage patterns.

We obtain this information through:

  • Direct submission from you.
  • Automatic collection during your website navigation. This automatically collected data may include usage statistics, IP addresses, and information gathered through cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking tools.
  • Third-party sources, including our business associates.

Information You Provide to Us

The information we gather on or via our Website may encompass:

  • Details you provide when completing Website forms. This includes information submitted during Website registration, service subscription, content posting, or additional service requests. We might also request information when you participate in our contests or promotions, or when you report Website issues.
  • Documentation and copies of your communications (including email addresses) when you contact us.
  • Your feedback on research surveys we may ask you to complete.
  • Information about transactions conducted through our Website and order fulfillment. Financial information may be required before processing Website orders.
  • Your Website search history.

You may also share information intended for publication or display (hereinafter, “posted”) in public Website areas, or for sharing with other Website users or third parties (collectively, “User Contributions”). Your User Contributions are shared and posted at your own discretion. While we restrict access to certain pages and allow you to configure privacy settings through your account profile, please understand that no security measures are completely foolproof. Furthermore, we cannot regulate how other Website users who have access to your User Contributions might use them. Therefore, we cannot guarantee protection against unauthorized viewing of your User Contributions.

Information We Collect Through Automatic Data Collection Technologies

While you browse and interact with our Website, we may employ automatic data collection technologies to gather specific information about:

  • Your Website visit details, including traffic patterns, location data, logs, other communication data, and your usage of Website resources.
  • Your computer and internet connection specifics, including IP address, operating system, and browser type.

We may also utilize these technologies to collect information about your online activities across time and on third-party websites or other online services (behavioral tracking).

The automatically collected information may contain personal data, which we might maintain independently or link with personal information acquired through other means or from third parties. This helps enhance our Website and deliver more personalized services by allowing us to:

  • Calculate our audience demographics and usage trends.
  • Save your preference information, enabling us to tailor our Website to match your personal interests.
  • Enhance search functionality.
  • Identify you upon subsequent Website visits.

For automatic data collection, we employ various technologies including:

  • Cookies (or browser cookies). These are small files stored on your computer’s hard drive. While you can decline browser cookies by adjusting your browser settings accordingly, this choice might restrict your access to certain Website features. Our system automatically issues cookies when you visit our Website unless you’ve modified your browser settings to reject them.
  • Web Beacons. Our Website pages and emails may incorporate small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These enable the Company to perform various functions, such as monitoring page visitor counts, tracking email open rates, and collecting other website-related statistics (including measuring content popularity and verifying system and server integrity).
  • Flash Cookies. Select Website features may utilize local stored objects (or Flash cookies) to gather and store information about your preferences and Website navigation patterns. Unlike browser cookies, Flash cookies are managed through different browser settings. For guidance on managing Flash cookie privacy and security settings, please refer to the Choices About How We Use and Disclose Your Information section.

Third-Party Use of Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies

Third parties, including advertisers, ad networks and servers, content providers, and application providers, may serve certain Website content or applications. These parties might employ cookies independently or alongside web beacons or other tracking technologies to collect information during your Website visits. They may associate this data with your personal information or gather details about your online behavior across time, websites, and other online services. This information helps them deliver targeted advertising or other customized content.

We don’t control these third parties’ tracking technologies or their implementation methods. For questions about advertisements or other targeted content, please contact the respective provider directly. To learn about opting out of targeted advertising from various providers, consult the Choices About How We Use and Disclose Your Information section.

How We Use Your Information

We utilize the information we collect about you or that you provide to us, including any personal information:

  • To showcase our Website and its complete range of contents to you.
  • To deliver requested information, products, or services that align with your specific needs.
  • To accomplish any additional purposes for which you’ve shared your information.
  • To send important notifications regarding your account/subscription status, including renewal reminders and expiration alerts.
  • To meet our contractual obligations and exercise our rights stemming from any agreements between us, including matters related to billing and payment collection.
  • To keep you informed about any modifications to our Website or updates to our products and services offerings.
  • To enable your participation in our Website’s various interactive elements.
  • To utilize the information in any other manner we specify at the time of collection.
  • To serve any other purposes with your explicit permission.

We may utilize your information to communicate with you about our products and services, as well as those of third parties that might interest you. Should you prefer not to receive such communications, you can either select the appropriate opt-out box on our data collection forms (such as registration or order forms) or modify your preferences within your account settings. For additional details, please refer to the Choices About How We Use and Disclose Your Information section.

We might leverage your collected information to display targeted advertisements to specific audience segments for our advertisers. While we don’t share your personal information for such purposes without your consent, advertisers may infer that you match their target criteria if you interact with their advertisements.

Disclosure of Your Information

We reserve the right to share aggregated, non-personally identifiable information about our users without any restrictions.

We may disclose personal information that we collect or you provide, as outlined in this privacy policy:

  • To our affiliated companies and subsidiaries.
  • To our business partners, contractors, and service providers who support our operations and are bound by confidentiality agreements to use personal information solely for designated purposes.
  • To acquiring entities or successors in cases of corporate transactions such as mergers, sales, restructuring, reorganization, dissolution, or asset transfers, where user personal information may be among the transferred assets.
  • To third-party marketers (with your consent or absent opt-out) for their promotional purposes, subject to confidentiality agreements restricting information use to specified purposes. For more details, see Choices About How We Use and Disclose Your Information.
  • To fulfill specific purposes you’ve authorized. For instance, if you use our “email a friend” feature, we’ll transmit your message content and email address to your designated recipients.
  • To serve any other purposes we disclose when you provide the information.
  • With your explicit consent.

We may also disclose your personal information:

  • To comply with any judicial orders, legal requirements, or regulatory processes, including responding to governmental or regulatory inquiries.
  • To implement or apply our terms of use, service terms, and other contractual agreements, particularly concerning billing and collection activities.
  • If we determine that disclosure is essential or appropriate to safeguard our rights, property, or security, as well as those of our customers and others. This encompasses sharing information with other entities and organizations to combat fraud and reduce credit risks.

Choices About How We Use and Disclose Your Information

We are committed to offering you control over your personal information shared with us. We’ve established mechanisms that provide you with the following options regarding your information:

  • Tracking Technologies and Advertising. Your browser settings can be adjusted to decline all or certain browser cookies, or to notify you when cookies are being deployed. For managing Flash cookie preferences, visit Adobe’s website to access Flash player settings. Please be aware that declining or disabling cookies may result in certain site features becoming unavailable or functioning improperly.
  • Disclosure of Your Information for Third-Party Advertising. To prevent us from sharing your personal information with independent or non-agent third parties for promotional activities, you may opt-out by emailing your request to info@ati-businessessentials.com.
  • Targeted Advertising. If you prefer that we don’t use your collected or provided information for delivering advertisements based on our advertisers’ target-audience preferences, you can opt-out by emailing your request to info@ati-businessessentials.com.
  • While we don’t control third parties’ collection or use of your information for interest-based advertising, these parties typically provide options to opt out of such data collection or usage. You can decline targeted ads from Network Advertising Initiative (“NAI”) members through their website.

If you reside in states like California, Nevada, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, or Utah, you may have additional rights and choices regarding your personal information. Refer to Your State Privacy Rights for detailed information.

Accessing and Correcting Your Information

You can email us at info@ati-businessessentials.com to request access to, modification of, or deletion of any personal information you’ve provided. Note that we cannot remove your personal information without also deleting your user account. We may decline information modification requests if we believe such changes would violate legal requirements or result in incorrect information.

Residents of states including California, Nevada, Colorado, Virginia, and Utah may be entitled to additional personal information rights and choices. Please consult Your State Privacy Rights for more details.

Your State Privacy Rights

Various state consumer privacy laws may grant their residents supplementary rights concerning our usage of their personal information.

California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and Utah extend specific privacy rights to their residents, including:

  • Verification of whether their personal information undergoes processing.
  • Rights to access and remove specified personal information.
  • Data portability privileges.
  • The ability to decline personal data processing for targeted advertising and sales purposes.

Additionally, Colorado, Connecticut, and Virginia residents are granted further rights, including:

  • The ability to rectify inaccuracies in their personal information, considering both the nature of the information and its processing purpose.
  • The option to decline profiling activities that result in legal or similarly significant decisions.

To exercise any of these privacy rights, please reach out to us at info@ati-businessessentials.com. Please note that we currently do not engage in data sales that would trigger statutory opt-out requirements.

Changes to Our Privacy Policy

We maintain a policy of posting all privacy policy modifications on this page. Should we implement substantial changes regarding our users’ personal information handling, we will inform you through updates to this page. The policy’s last revision date is shown at the top. It is your responsibility to ensure we have your current, active email address and to regularly review our Website and privacy policy for any updates.

Contact Information

For questions or comments regarding this privacy policy and our privacy practices, please contact us at info@ati-businessessentials.com.
