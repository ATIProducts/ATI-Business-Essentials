What is Leads Plus?

Leads Plus is a lead and bid tracking platform from ATI Business Essentials built for contractors and service businesses that win work through bids and estimates. It captures website form leads and phone calls automatically, tracks bid amounts and status, and sends qualified-lead data back to Google Ads so your ads find more customers like your best ones.

  • Starts at $299/month plus $699 onboarding
  • Unlimited users, works in any web browser
  • Pairs with managed Google Ads for a complete lead engine

Leads Plus FAQ

Is Leads Plus a CRM?

No. Leads Plus is a focused lead and bid tracking system. It skips the contact-management bloat of a full CRM and only tracks what service businesses need: incoming leads, calls, bid amounts, job types, and win or loss status.

How does Leads Plus improve Google Ads results?

When you mark a lead as qualified, Leads Plus sends that conversion back to Google Ads along with the dollar value of the quote. Google then optimizes toward people who look like your qualified leads instead of anyone who fills out a form, which lowers cost per qualified lead over time.

How much does Leads Plus cost?

Leads Plus with a website connection is $299 per month. Leads Plus with website and call tracking is $349 per month. Both include a one-time $699 onboarding fee, unlimited users, and up to 3 edits per month. Bundling with managed Google Ads saves over 10%.

Who is Leads Plus built for?

Contractors and service businesses that quote or bid before winning a job, including roofers, scaffolding companies, general contractors, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, excavation, landscaping, and dozens of other trades across the United States.

Do I need to change my website to use Leads Plus?

No. ATI connects your existing website forms during onboarding. If you do not want to connect your site, ATI can build a standalone landing page that feeds Leads Plus for $999 setup and $65 per month.
