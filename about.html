Who we are

Who is ATI Business Essentials?

ATI Business Essentials, LLC is a marketing and software company in Arvada, Colorado, and a wholly owned subsidiary of ATI Products, LLC. It builds Leads Plus and Marketing Hub, manages Google Ads, and provides safety compliance and website services for service businesses across the United States. It was founded by Darren Franz and Sabra Starr.

What We Stand For

Simplicity

We build tools that service businesses can actually use, without a training manual.

Service Industry Focus

We only work with businesses that run on bids and estimates.

Results First

Every feature exists to help you close more jobs and waste less money.

Transparency

Clear reporting, honest strategy, and no long-term contracts.

Why We Built Leads Plus

We watched service business owners struggle with bloated CRMs, disconnected marketing, and Google Ads that sent junk leads. So we built a simpler way: a focused lead and bid tracking system that makes Google smarter over time.

Meet the Founding Partners

Darren Franz

Founding Partner

Darren built the company with a clear vision: create a simple, results-driven system that helps service businesses grow. With years of hands-on experience alongside contractors and business owners, he understands what it takes to generate consistent, qualified work. His focus is strategy, long-term relationships, and systems that scale.

Sabra Starr

Founding Partner

Sabra leads development and day-to-day execution of the platform, including the creation of Marketing Hub and the systems that support client growth. She works directly with business owners to capture leads, manage marketing, and turn opportunities into consistent work. Her focus is simple, organized tools that make marketing easier and more effective.
