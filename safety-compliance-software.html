Quick answer

What is SiteDocs?

SiteDocs is a digital safety management platform that replaces paper forms for contractors. It handles FLRAs, JSAs, toolbox talks, inspections, and OSHA 300 logs, sends automatic certification alerts, and works offline on job sites. Booking a SiteDocs demo through ATI Business Essentials includes an automatic 10% discount.

Paper-Free Safety Compliance, From Small Crews to Multinationals

SiteDocs replaces cumbersome paper systems with a streamlined digital platform for contractors across the United States. Smaller firms cut the administrative burden of manual paperwork, while large organizations use its SOC 2 certified data security and real-time dashboards to spot injury trends and improve site performance.

By digitizing documents like FLRAs, JSAs, and OSHA 300 logs, SiteDocs customers have reported saving over $100,000 in administrative costs in a single year and a 22.5% decrease in common workplace injuries, according to SiteDocs. Crews adapt quickly, data processing takes days instead of weeks, and safety culture becomes proactive on every job site.

SiteDocs FAQ

Does SiteDocs work without internet on a job site?

Yes. Crews can keep filling out forms offline, and SiteDocs syncs automatically when the device reconnects.

What safety documents can SiteDocs digitize?

Field level risk assessments (FLRAs), job safety analyses (JSAs), toolbox talks and safety meetings, inspections, certifications, and OSHA 300 logs, among others.
