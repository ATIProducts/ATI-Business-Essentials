What are OSHAWise and MSHAWise?

OSHAWise and MSHAWise are AI-powered compliance research platforms. OSHAWise covers OSHA standards and MSHAWise covers mine safety (MSHA) rules. Each combines regulations, official interpretations, and case law in one searchable tool with AI copilots that answer compliance questions in seconds. OSHAWise & MSHAWise FAQ

What is the difference between OSHAWise and MSHAWise?

OSHAWise covers Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) standards. MSHAWise covers Mine Safety and Health Administration (MSHA) rules for mining operations. Both include regulations, interpretations, and case law.

How much do OSHAWise and MSHAWise cost?

Each subscription is $228 when purchased through ATI Business Essentials.

Who should use OSHAWise or MSHAWise?

Safety directors, compliance managers, contractors, and mine operators who need fast, reliable answers about what a regulation requires and how it has been enforced.
